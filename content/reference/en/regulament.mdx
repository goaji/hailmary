## The Objective of the Game

*(Stub content — full text lands at step 5.)* Two teams of 11 players try to advance the ball into the opponent's territory and score points, mainly through touchdowns and field goals. Whoever has more points at the end wins.

## The Field and Its Dimensions

*(Stub.)* The field is 100 yards long between the goal lines, plus a 10-yard end zone at each end, and 53.3 yards wide.

## Game Length and the Clock

*(Stub.)* A game has four 15-minute quarters, with a break at halftime.

## The Set of 4 Downs

*(Stub.)* The offense gets four attempts to advance the ball at least ten yards.

## The Forward Pass

*(Stub.)* American football is the only major sport where the ball can be thrown forward, unlike rugby — but only once per play, and only from behind the line of scrimmage.

## How Points Are Scored

*(Stub.)* Touchdown (6 points), extra point (1 or 2 points), field goal (3 points), safety (2 points).

## Offensive Positions

*(Stub.)* Quarterback, running back, wide receiver, tight end and the offensive line.

## Defensive Positions

*(Stub.)* The defensive line, linebackers and the secondary (cornerback, safety).

## Common Penalties

*(Stub.)* Offside, holding, pass interference and false start are the ones seen most often on TV.

## Special Teams

*(Stub.)* The units responsible for kickoffs, punts and field goals.
