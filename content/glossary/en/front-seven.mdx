The front seven is the collective name for the four (or three) defensive linemen plus the three (or four) linebackers — essentially the whole defense except the secondary. They're the players closest to the line of scrimmage, mainly responsible for stopping the run and pressuring the quarterback.

The exact split varies by scheme — a 4-3 defense uses four linemen and three linebackers, a 3-4 flips that ratio — but the total stays seven. A dominant front seven can make a defense look good even with an average secondary, since constant quarterback pressure disrupts any passing attack's rhythm.
