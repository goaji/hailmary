A fumble happens when the player carrying the ball loses control of it — usually from a hard tackle — before the referee whistles the play dead. The ball is live on the field, and either team can recover it.

A defensive fumble recovery is one of the most valuable plays in the game, since it instantly flips possession without the offense finishing its set of downs. That's why players drill hard on securing the ball tightly against contact.

A fumble is different from an interception: an interception happens during a pass in the air, while a fumble occurs after the ball was already in a player's possession on the field.
