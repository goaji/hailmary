The secondary is the collective name for the cornerbacks and safeties, the players positioned furthest from the line of scrimmage. Their main job is pass coverage — stopping opposing receivers from catching the ball, especially on longer routes.

Unlike the front seven (the defensive line and linebackers), who deal primarily with stopping the run and pressuring the quarterback, the secondary takes center stage on passing plays. A weak secondary can turn an average passing offense into a devastating one, no matter how good the rest of the defense is.

Commentators often talk about the secondary getting "beat" — a moment when coverage breaks down and a receiver comes open for a long completion.
